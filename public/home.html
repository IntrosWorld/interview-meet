A few things
before you begin.

What can I practice?

Start a voice interview for a role such as Software Engineer, Product Manager, or Data Analyst. For coding, choose a supported language and the topic you want to work on.

Do I need a camera?

No. The camera is optional. Voice interviews use your microphone, and you can also send typed answers during a session.

Can I revisit my interviews?

Yes. Sign in to save your sessions and view their transcripts under My interviews. You can delete sessions from your history.
